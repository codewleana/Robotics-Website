History of the Furious Falcons

The Furious Falcons were founded in 2012 with the goal of building a strong robotics program at Foster High School, later expanding to include students from Tomas High School.

Since then, we've grown into a competitive team that has taken part in regional and national events, earning recognition for innovative designs and a strong work ethic.

Who the Furious Falcons Are

The Furious Falcons are a high school robotics team spanning Foster and Tomas High Schools in Richmond, Texas. We build competition robots and engage with our community along the way.

We believe in the power of STEM education and work to create a supportive environment where students can learn, grow, and excel in robotics.
