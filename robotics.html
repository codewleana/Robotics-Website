History of the Furious Falcons

The Furious Falcons were founded in 2012 with the goal of creating a strong robotics program at Foster High School, which later expanded into Tomas High School.

Over the years, we have grown into a successful team that has competed in various regional and national competitions, earning recognition for our innovative designs and strong work ethic.

Who the Furious Falcons Are

The Furious Falcons are a high school robotics team based in Foster and Tomas High Schools located in Richmond, Texas. We are dedicated to building innovative robots for competition and community engagement.

We believe in the power of STEM education and strive to create a supportive environment where students can learn, grow, and excel in the field of robotics.
